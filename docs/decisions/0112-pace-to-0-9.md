# 0112. Pace to 0.9: the D3 freeze lifted, the open pull request limit suspended, S1 in 0.5

## Context

The first `:stable` promotion on evidence ran on 2026-10-14 (#146, promote-stable run
38546669761), the exit of release 0.3. The maintainer asked to reach release 0.9 as soon as
possible, with work on several fronts at once.

Four facts shape that work:

- The freeze of [ADR-0103](0103-audit-5-decisions.md) D3 holds specification revisions and
  non-critical shell work until P2 (#122) and spike S1 (#124) are green. P2 closed on
  2026-10-10. S1 has not run: it needs a Fedora 45 virtual machine with Secure Boot and swtpm,
  about six hours of machine time, and its probe plan (`docs/spikes/s1-bootc-fedora45.md`) is
  written. The graphical work of 0.9 waits on it without depending on its result.
- `AGENTS.md` allows at most five open pull requests. Four were open, two of them from the
  bump bots.
- S1 sits in the contents of 0.3 (ADR-0105), whose exit, the first `:stable` promotion on
  evidence, has already happened; only P4b of 0.5 needs S1's result.
- The lock (`doc_lock_and_prompts.md` LP18), portal (`doc_portal.md` PT18) and visual
  language (VL12) plans ask the maintainer to judge every step from step 2 on, on the
  reference laptop, while ADR-0105 puts all hardware work in one session at 0.9.

## Decision

Decided by the maintainer on 2026-10-14, as recommended except item 2.

1. **The D3 freeze is lifted.** Specification revisions and shell work run in parallel with
   S1, within the machine's CPU budget. S1 still gates P4b (#126), as `doc_kernel_profile.md`
   section 15 says, and with it release 0.5 (`release-1.0.md` section 4.1).
2. **The limit of five open pull requests is suspended until the 0.9 tag is signed.** No
   limit applies until then; the limit of five returns when the signed 0.9 tag is pushed,
   whether or not its `:stable` promotion has run. The recommendation was a limit of ten.
   Every other part of the rule holds: reviewers' findings on open pull requests are closed
   before new work on the same area, and a branch without an open pull request is merged,
   archived or deleted.
3. **The four releases of [ADR-0105](0105-releases-before-1-0.md) stay.** Each 0.x is a signed
   tag on `iso-v0` and a `:stable` promotion on evidence, with the `release` approval. The
   24-hour dwell of one release runs while the next one is built.
4. **S1 (#124) moves from 0.3 to 0.5.** Release 0.3 is tagged once its remaining issues are
   closed, without waiting for S1; S1 joins the contents of 0.5 beside P4b, which it gates.
5. **The dev VM stands in for the reference laptop until the 0.9 hardware session.** Where
   LP18, PT18 or VL12 ask the maintainer to judge a step on the reference laptop, the step
   proceeds on the dev VM with screenshots or recordings for the maintainer's judgement; the
   judgements on hardware are made in the single hardware session of 0.9 (ADR-0105). The
   decision covers these three plans only: OD17, LN19, FR21, FM21 and the notification centre
   plan make the same demand and keep it until the maintainer decides on them.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Keep the freeze until S1 is green.** It holds the graphical work of 0.9, the longest
  chain left, behind a spike that does not change it.
- **Tag only 0.3 and 0.9.** It saves two approvals and two dwell periods, which run in
  parallel with other work anyway, and loses two end-to-end proofs of the update chain on
  real content.

## Consequences

- `release-1.0.md` section 4 records the freeze as lifted and moves S1 from the 0.3 row to
  the 0.5 row; issue #124 moves to milestone 0.5. `AGENTS.md` records the suspension and
  its end.
- The laptop steps of LP18, PT18 and VL12 read "the dev VM, until the 0.9 hardware
  session" by this record; their documents are not revised for it.
- More pull requests wait on the maintainer's review at once. Each still goes through the
  merge rule of `CLAUDE.md`; nothing in this record changes who approves.
- ADR-0103 D3 and ADR-0105 item 5 (the freeze) and its 0.3 contents (S1) are amended by
  this record.
