## 1. Scope, non-goals and rationale

### Scope

`athanor-osd`, one resident program of the session: the transient overlay that confirms what a key did (volume and microphone mute, screen and keyboard backlight, keyboard layout, airplane mode, the touchpad switch and the media keys), the end-of-session dialogs (log out, restart, shut down), monitor identification and the headset or headphone choice dialog; its interface `os.athanor.Osd1`; the path a key takes to it under athanor-sulphur and in cosmic-comp's session; the models it reuses; its surfaces, placement, contents, visual language, accessibility, privileges, tests and order of construction; and the one step in which cosmic-osd leaves the image.

### Non-goals

Caps Lock and Num Lock (M3) and the other exclusions of OD14; the polkit agent and every password, which belong to `doc_lock_and_prompts.md` (D12); feedback for keys pressed on the lock screen; the screen-reader key (`doc_accessibility.md`) and the display toggle (the display settings); a prompt to force-close an unresponsive application and hold-to-confirm (OD18); key bindings a user configures, which athanor-sulphur does not read today (`doc_own_stack.md` OS13); and the drawing toolkit and the compositor themselves, whose requirements this document states (OD17) and whose own documents settle.

## 2. Interfaces and decisions

**OD1. One program owns the keys, their feedback and the end-of-session dialogs** (M1, D12). A new program, `athanor-osd`, receives each key's action, carries it out through the models of `athanor-services`, and shows the result; it also draws the log-out, restart and shut-down dialogs (OD18), the numbers that identify monitors and the headset or headphone choice dialog (OD19). The program that acts is the program that shows, so the overlay shows the value the service confirmed, with no second process to keep in step.

- **Resident and hidden**, as the launcher and the control center: one process per session, started by `athanor-osd.service` in `athanor-session.target` by preset, with no surface mapped while nothing is shown.
- **Its own models**, as CC2 requires of each process: it starts the audio, battery, network and media models of `athanor-services` on its own Tokio runtime, and the compositor client's output, workspace and keyboard-group models. Revised (proposed, revision 3): the models start without GTK, from `athanor-services` itself (requirement C1: the start of `athanor-controls/src/services.rs:58` moves into `athanor-services` with no change of behaviour, and `athanor-controls` keeps only its GLib follow loop, `bridge.rs`); their watch channels reach Sal's loop through a calloop channel given to `athanor_sal::run_with` (`driver.rs:266`), and the timers of OD10, OD11, OD18 and OD19 run on the Tokio side and arrive the same way.

**OD2. Crates.**

- `forge/specs/athanor-osd`: the program. Revised: it depends on `athanor-sal` (maintainer decision, 2026-11-06, ADR-0138: Sal, not GTK4) and, as proposed by revision 3 following ADR-0138, on `athanor-services`, `athanor-sulphur-client` without default features and with `calloop`, as the toolkit dock does (`forge/specs/athanor-dock/athanor-dock-1.0.0/Cargo.toml:27`, `:32`), `athanor-style` without default features, `athanor-i18n`, `athanor-unit`, `tokio` and `zbus`; not on `athanor-controls`, `gtk4` or `gtk4-layer-shell`. **OD4. The interface `os.athanor.Osd1`.**

- **Methods.** Those without arguments return nothing: the twelve of OD3's table, plus `KeyboardBrightnessUp` and `KeyboardBrightnessDown` of the new rows of `DEFAULTS`, `CycleKeyboardBrightness`, `ToggleAirplane` and `AskRestart` (the bar's power menu, OD18), and `IdentifyOutputs` and `DismissOutputIdentifiers` (OD19). Four methods take an argument: `ConfirmHeadset(u device)` opens the headset or headphone dialog for a PipeWire device (OD19), and `ShowKeyboardAid(s notice)`, which shows a sticky, slow or bounce key notice (`doc_accessibility.md` AX11); `notice` is one of a fixed set of names and anything else is refused. `SetOutputBrightness(s make, s model, s serial, u percent)` sets the brightness of one monitor and `GetOutputBrightness(s make, s model, s serial) -> u percent` reads it (names proposed, revision 4, after `IdentifyOutputs` and `KeyboardBrightnessUp`; the monitor is three string arguments, not one `(sss)` struct, as `ShowKeyboardAid(s notice)` and `ConfirmHeadset(u device)` take plain arguments; the next bullets give their rules). A method acts and shows, or opens a dialog that asks, and returns nothing but its outcome, with one declared exception: only `GetOutputBrightness` returns state, so the interface reveals nothing else a caller could not see on screen.
- **Admission.** Any peer on the session bus may call. Every method does what the person can already do from the keyboard, the bar or the control center, under the target service's own policy: logind admits `SetBrightness` from the active session (CC8), NetworkManager its radio switches, PipeWire its volume; the `Ask` methods only open a dialog, and nothing ends the session until the person confirms in it or lets its countdown run. A consequence accepted by the maintainer on 2026-10-08 (M8): an unconfined process of the user can call `AskPowerOff` and, with nobody at the keyboard, the machine powers off when the countdown ends; under `doc_threat_model.md` TM1 such a process is the user, and logind already lets the active session power off without the dialog. A Flatpak application cannot reach the name, which `xdg-dbus-proxy` does not expose. The caller's cgroup is logged when it is not the compositor's (athanor-sulphur's, whose `session-calls` worker is a thread of the compositor, or cosmic-comp's) or the bar's, as BR1's informative check. `ShowKeyboardAid`, `ConfirmHeadset`, `SetOutputBrightness` and `GetOutputBrightness` are the exceptions; the brightness pair is admitted as the bullet on output brightness below says. `ShowKeyboardAid` is admitted only from the cgroup of `athanor-a11y.service`, checked through the caller's pidfd as `athanor-shelld` checks its callers, because a notice from any other peer would misstate the keyboard's state. `ConfirmHeadset` is admitted only from the cgroup of `cosmic-settings-daemon.service` until step 8 of the session-daemons construction, and from the cgroup of `athanor-sessiond.service` from that step (`doc_session_daemons.md` SD15, SD22), by the same check, because it takes the keyboard (OD19); a peer that is not the daemon would otherwise be able to grab the keyboard with a dialog.
- **Rate.** Except `SetOutputBrightness`, which the next bullet coalesces instead, more than 30 calls within one second from one sender are dropped beyond the thirtieth, with one journal line; a held key never reaches this, since it runs once in cosmic-comp's session (section 1) and, if spike O1 confirms it, under athanor-sulphur.
- **Output brightness** (`SetOutputBrightness`, `GetOutputBrightness`), for the control center's per-monitor slider, which reaches the bus only through `athanor-osd` as a client of `Osd1` (`doc_session_daemons.md` SD2, role 12):
  - **Caller** (maintainer decision, 2026-11-06). Only the control center's unit may call the two methods. `athanor-osd` checks the caller by its cgroup, the exact unit of the control center, as the lock's PAM helper checks its peer (`doc_lock_and_prompts.md` LP8). Any other peer gets `org.freedesktop.DBus.Error.AccessDenied` (proposed, revision 4).
  - **How the check is made** (proposed, revision 4), following LP8 (`doc_lock_and_prompts.md:123`). The caller's process is resolved through its pidfd, its cgroup path is read and compared whole with that of `athanor-control-center.service`, never by suffix or prefix, and the pidfd is checked again afterwards to still name a live process, so a recycled pid cannot pass. The check is a requirement of this bullet, not an informative one as BR1's is. The residual risk is accepted: any process of the same uid can start a transient unit of that name with `systemd-run --user` and pass; under `doc_threat_model.md` TM1 such an unconfined process is the user, and already has `uaccess` on the i2c-dev nodes (SD2, role 12). A Flatpak application cannot reach the name.
  - **Value** (maintainer decision, 2026-11-06). The set takes any value from 0 to 100 % in one call, where a key moves by a step (OD6). The guard of the lowest step stays (`doc_session_daemons.md` SD2, role 13, OD15), so the set never turns a monitor off. This is a declared exception to OD12's rule that no method does more than a keyboard can, echoed in OD4.
  - **Range and the zero** (proposed, revision 4). A value above 100 gets `org.freedesktop.DBus.Error.InvalidArgs`. A 0 is clamped to the lowest step, and the get and the signal report the value actually written. Role 13 defines the guard on a backlight's `actual_brightness`, which a monitor does not have, so on DDC/CI the lowest step is one raw unit of VCP feature 0x10 (the maximum comes from the Get reply), the write is clamped before it is made, and role 13's read-back loop is not carried over.
  - **Pace** (maintainer decision, 2026-11-06). The latest value wins: `athanor-osd` coalesces the set calls of one monitor and writes only the latest value to the bus, at the pace the monitor allows. The cap of 30 calls per second stays for the other methods.
  - **The cap on the get** (proposed, revision 4). The cap applies to `GetOutputBrightness`, as the reading of "the other methods" gives.
  - **The reply of the set** (proposed, revision 4). The set replies after the write that applied its value, or after a later write that superseded it, and returns that write's outcome, so a failed transaction reaches the slider as `Failed`.
  - **Overlay** (maintainer decision, 2026-11-06). A change from the slider shows no overlay; a brightness key, which reaches the same model, does (OD5). This agrees with OD5's backlight exception, which exists only because a write through logind cannot be told from the firmware's.
  - **State returned.** `GetOutputBrightness` is the first method that returns state: the current brightness of one monitor, as a percentage, which the person can read on the monitor's own menu. It is admitted from the control center's unit alone, like the set, so the exception reaches no other peer (proposed, revision 4), inferred from the caller decision. A transaction with the monitor is made only after the EDID gate of `doc_session_daemons.md` SD2, role 12, which is not restated here.
  - **Identity** (maintainer decision, 2026-11-06). A monitor is identified by its make, model and serial, as the control center sees them from Wayland. `athanor-osd` matches them against the EDID it already reads at 0x50 before each burst (`doc_session_daemons.md` SD2, role 12), so the match needs no DRM connector and works on the PCI-child fallback of the NVIDIA adapters. Two identical monitors with no serial are never guessed: the call gets `NotSupported` (proposed, revision 4).
  - **How the strings are equal** (maintainer decision, 2026-11-06). A small Rust EDID parser is shared by Sulphur and `athanor-osd`. Sulphur applies it to the EDID blob property of the DRM connector, which exists on NVIDIA too, and reports make, model and serial through `wl_output` and wlr-output-management; `athanor-osd` applies it to the bytes it reads at 0x50. The strings are therefore equal by construction. Today they are not: Sulphur sends make `Athanor`, model `DRM` and an empty serial for every DRM output (`forge/specs/athanor-sulphur/athanor-sulphur-1.0.0/src/udev.rs:807-810`, `src/state.rs:1469-1475`), because smithay-drm-extras is built without libdisplay-info (`Cargo.toml:18-19`). A Sulphur pull request that applies the parser lands before the slider's.
  - **What the parser returns** (proposed, revision 4). The make is the three-letter PNP ID of the EDID, not a name mapped from it, so no table has to be kept identical on both sides. The model is the monitor-name descriptor (tag 0xFC), else the product code in hexadecimal. The serial is the serial descriptor string (tag 0xFF), else the 32-bit serial number when it is not zero, else the empty string, which means "no serial". An EDID that cannot be parsed gives `NotSupported`.
  - **When the match runs** (proposed, revision 4). The EDID of each candidate bus is parsed once per output change (a hotplug or a resume) and the identities are cached; the per-burst EDID gate of role 12 stays as it is. A monitor that answered `NotSupported` is tried again on the next output change.
  - **Where the parser lives** (proposed, revision 4). In a new crate named `athanor-edid`, in the workspace, a library with no I/O that Sulphur and `athanor-osd` both depend on. Its inventory entry in `docs/architecture/components.toml` and its manifest belong to the pull request that adds it.
  - **Notification** (maintainer decision, 2026-11-06). The signal `OutputBrightnessChanged(s make, s model, s serial, u percent)` reports a change of a monitor's brightness. It is sent with a unicast destination, only to the connection of the admitted control center; no other peer sees a brightness.
  - **What the signal covers** (proposed, revision 4). A change from a key and one from a set both send it. The get is admitted from the control center alone.
  - **The destination** (proposed, revision 4). `athanor-osd` takes the unique bus name of the most recent admitted caller, and clears it on `NameOwnerChanged` when that name loses its owner; with none, no signal is sent. The unicast limits the signal to the honest peers, not to an adversary: under TM1 a process of the same uid can use `BecomeMonitor` and read every message on the session bus, and the value is not a secret, since the monitor's own menu shows it.
  - **Errors** (maintainer decision, 2026-11-06). Two distinct D-Bus errors. `NotSupported` covers a monitor with no DDC/CI brightness and a bus that fails the EDID gate; the control center hides that monitor's slider. `Failed` covers a failed transaction; the slider stays and shows that the last change did not apply.
  - **The names and the other cases** (proposed, revision 4). `Osd1` has no error namespace of its own, so the standard names `org.freedesktop.DBus.Error.NotSupported` and `.Failed` are used. An identity that matches no monitor, or more than one, gets `NotSupported`; a value above 100, `InvalidArgs`; a caller that is not admitted, `AccessDenied`.
- **Name.** `athanor-osd` owns `os.athanor.Osd1`; a second instance that cannot take the name exits. It owns no COSMIC bus name: nothing installed addresses `com.system76.CosmicOnScreenDisplay` or cosmic-osd 1.11's `com.system76.CosmicOsd` (section 1, OD19).

**OD5. What shows, and when** (M2).

- **A key always shows**, also when the value does not change: volume up at the ceiling shows the full bar, so the person sees that the key worked.
- **A change the hardware made shows** when no shell control asked for it: the keyboard layout changed by any means (including a layout shortcut of XKB options), the screen backlight changed by the firmware (OD6), the keyboard backlight changed by the embedded controller, the radios blocked by a hardware switch.
- **A change made in a shell control does not show:** a slider of the bar, the control center or Settings is its own feedback. A change made by another application (a mixer) does not show either. The backlight is the exception below.
- **Two exceptions** (proposed, revision 3). A layout switch made from the bar's input-source module shows, because the keyboard group's `group` event carries no origin (section 1). A backlight change the program did not command, made by another program such as `brightnessctl` or by the control center's slider, which logind writes the same way, shows while `POLLPRI` alone reports the backlight (OD6), because it cannot tell either from the firmware's; that the slider's change shows is a deviation from M2, for the maintainer to approve (M18). If spike O2 finds the uevent's `SOURCE` readable under the unit's sandbox, only a change with `SOURCE=hotkey` shows, and neither of these does (open doubt 5).
- **The slider of an external monitor does not show** (maintainer decision, 2026-11-06), and the brightness key does: a set from the control center reaches `athanor-osd` as `SetOutputBrightness` (OD4), so the program knows its origin, which is what the backlight exception above cannot know. This is consistent with that exception.
- **Media keys show** the action (play, pause, next, previous) with the player's name, not the track; the track belongs to the media controls (F-osd-14 is excluded, OD14).
- **One overlay at a time.** A new kind replaces the one on screen in place, within the same frame budget; a repeat of the same kind updates the value in place. An open end-of-session dialog hides the overlay until it closes.

Screen brightness moves by 5 % of the maximum and never below one raw step, as the battery model already does (`battery.rs:533-558`). Keyboard brightness moves by one hardware level; `CycleKeyboardBrightness` wraps from the highest level to off.
- **Each key acts on the state it sees.** A key press while a previous command is still running is applied to the value that command will set, so five quick presses move the volume by 25 %, not by 5 %. The battery model's brightness coalescing already behaves this way.

**OD7. The overlay's surface.** One layer-shell surface per output, created once, pinned and always mapped, as `doc_launcher.md` LA8 requires (cosmic-comp disconnects a client that destroys, unmaps and remaps, or rebuilds an unpinned layer surface). Hidden, it holds no content, an empty input region, no keyboard interactivity and a size of 1×1 on the background layer; shown, it takes the layer `overlay` and the interactivity named below. Kept under athanor-sulphur (proposed, revision 3): one pattern serves both compositors while cosmic-comp's session exists, and a surface that is never destroyed costs nothing while hidden. Sal must gain it (requirement S1): the application opens and closes layer surfaces at run time, several on one output (the overlay, a dialog, an identification number), and `Shell::set_layer` changes the layer too, through `zwlr_layer_surface_v1.set_layer`, which athanor-sulphur accepts from a client with the grant (section 1).

- **Layer** `overlay`, so the overlay shows above a fullscreen video. **Keyboard interactivity** `none`, as the old shell's overlay already set it (`osd.rs:122-123`). **Exclusive zone** 0, which asks the compositor to move the surface clear of every surface with a positive exclusive zone, such as a bar at the bottom (`wlr-layer-shell-unstable-v1.xml:168-171`, wayland-protocols-wlr 0.3.12). **Input region** empty, so a pointer click passes through to the window beneath.
- **Drawn by Sal** (maintainer decision, 2026-11-06, ADR-0138), with `vello_cpu`, Sal's default renderer (proposed, revision 3, following ADR-0138), in place of revision 2's Cairo inside GTK4.
- **While the session is locked** the compositor shows only the lock surface, so the overlay does not appear; the keys the compositor still runs while locked act anyway (section 1). Feedback on the lock screen belongs to the lock specification.

**OD8. Placement.**

- **Output:** the output of the active workspace, as the bar's popups (BR4), read from the compositor client's workspace model; under athanor-sulphur through `ext_workspace_manager_v1`, which needs a grant (K1).
- **Position:** centred horizontally, anchored to the bottom edge with a margin of 48 px (eight steps of the grid), above any surface with an exclusive zone there (OD7). When the bar of that output is at the bottom and floats without an exclusive zone, the margin grows by the bar's thickness and its 6 px edge, read from `athanor-layout`.
- **The dock** is drawn in the top layer and may sit under the overlay while revealed; the overlay is in the overlay layer and is short-lived, so it wins.
- **Right-to-left:** the position does not change; the contents mirror (icon at the start edge, value at the end edge).

**OD10. Visual language.**

- **Shape:** the toolkit's `radius.panel`, 30 under revision 3 of the visual language (VL10; revision 2 said 24) (proposed, revision 3); padding 18; width fixed at 300 px for a level so it does not jump with the number, a state as wide as its label within 120 to 420 px.

**OD13. Crash, budget, idle.**

- **Crash policy** of SH8 and BR1: restarted by systemd after 1 s; after 5 failures within 10 minutes it stays down with one journal line. D-Bus activation (OD3) then fails as well, because systemd refuses to start a unit past its start limit: the keys do nothing until the next login, and the bench sees a failed unit. The bar's power menu then falls back to its own confirmation (section 3, BR3).
- **Recovery** (ST5): killed with `SIGKILL`, the next key press within 1 s of the kill shows its overlay. An overlay or a dialog on screen at the time of the kill does not reappear, and a dialog killed before its countdown ended ends nothing.
- **Memory:** at most 32 MB PSS at rest with no surface shown, after an hour of use. This is an estimate, made for a GTK process and kept for Sal (revision 3), not a ceiling: by ADR-0068 (A2-31) the ceiling is set at the value measured after an hour of use plus 15%, and the unit's `MemoryMax=` follows it (M9).
- **Idle:** hidden, the program holds no timer and wakes only on a model's signal or a call; the backlight signal (OD6) replaces the battery model's re-reading on every wake. The dialog's countdown timer exists only while a dialog is open.

### Failure behaviour

- **The program down** (OD13): restarted after 1 s; past its start limit the keys do nothing until the next login under either compositor, since the bus cannot activate the unit, and the bar's power menu confirms by itself (section 3, BR3). A kill is recovered within 1 s, and a dialog killed ends nothing.
- **A missing device or service** (proposed, revision 3): on a machine without a keyboard backlight, such as the maintainer's desktop (section 1), the keyboard-backlight keys show nothing, with one journal line at start and none per press; a key whose service is down shows nothing until the service returns.
- **A missing grant under athanor-sulphur:** without K1's line the compositor offers none of the privileged globals, and the pinned surface, which opens on the background layer without a grant, is unmapped and closed when it asks for the overlay layer (section 1). Sal today drops a layer surface the compositor closed without telling the application, and asks `Application::output` for a new one at that output's next change (`system/athanor-sal/src/driver.rs:434-437`, `:1412-1426`, `:1834-1840`), which the compositor closes again at its next show. S1 therefore includes telling the application that the compositor closed one of its surfaces; `athanor-osd` then logs it once, opens no other surface on that output, and shows nothing there, and the keys still act through their services. Without K2's global the touchpad switch still works and shows nothing.
- **The headset dialog without the daemon's socket** opens nothing and logs once (OD19).
- **A call while locked:** the overlay is not shown, `ConfirmHeadset`, `AskLogOut`, `AskRestart` and `AskPowerOff` are dropped with one journal line each (OD18, OD19), and athanor-sulphur drops a queued call without `while_locked` (section 1).
- **Above the rate** (OD4): calls beyond the thirtieth within one second from one sender are dropped, with one journal line.
- **An inhibitor** that blocks shutdown stops the countdown, and the dialog says so (OD18).

## 6. Decisions taken

Taken by the maintainer on 2026-10-05.

- **M1. Which process owns the overlay and the keys** (OD1, OD2). **A:** a new program, `athanor-osd`, that acts and shows. Reason: one process per surface (SH4), a crash of the bar does not take the feedback, and the value shown is the one its own model confirmed.
- **M2. When the overlay shows** (OD5). **A:** keys and changes made by the hardware, never changes made in a shell control. Reason: it shows what the person did without echoing what they are already watching.
- **M3. Caps Lock and Num Lock** (OD14, F-osd-10). **A:** request a lock-state event upstream and exclude the indicator from 1.0. Reason: polling the LED files contradicts the idle rule, and the state is already visible in the text typed.
- **M4. Coexistence with cosmic-osd** (OD15, OD17). **A:** hold the handover until cosmic-osd can leave, and ship both together. Reason: no patch on a package that is leaving, and no duplicate overlays for users.
- **M5. The cosmic-settings-daemon defect on the shipped image** (section 1, OD15). **B, amended by M6:** no patch before the retirement step; `athanor-osd` removes the volume keys' dependence on the daemon. At the retirement step the one-line fix of the daemon's unit ships with OD19, because the headset dialog needs the daemon's audio socket bound (M6). Reason: the maintainer chose not to patch a unit whose key role leaves in the retirement step, and M6 later made the fix part of that step. Amended (maintainer decision 2026-10-15, #144): the fix shipped early, in #198 (`ReadWritePaths=%t` in `cosmic-settings-daemon.service`), so "no patch before the retirement step" no longer holds; `athanor-osd` still removes the volume keys' dependence on the daemon.
- **M6. Monitor identification and the headset dialog at the retirement step** (OD19, OD15, OD17, F-osd-08, F-osd-09). Decided by the maintainer on 2026-10-05 as a follow-up, **against this draft's recommendation** (which was to exclude both, leave them `missing` at the retirement step and give them to the display page of stage 6 and to stage 8). **A:** `athanor-osd` also draws both when cosmic-osd leaves, so there is no gap. Consequences recorded in OD19: the `cosmic-osd` client link, `ConfirmHeadset` admitted from the daemon's cgroup only, a small varlink call in the audio model, and the fix of cosmic-settings-daemon's unit in the retirement step, which M5 had left unpatched. Amended (maintainer decision 2026-10-15, #144): that fix shipped early, in #198 (M5).
- **D7 (lock and prompts, joint). How cosmic-osd stops being the polkit agent** (OD15). **A:** cosmic-osd leaves with `athanor-osd` and `athanor-polkit-agent` in one step, with no patch. Reason: relying on registration order is a race, and one step removes the race and the duplicate overlays together.
- **D12 (lock and prompts). Who owns the end-of-session dialogs** (OD18). **A:** this document, with cosmic-osd's replacement; polkit asks the lock specification's agent for any password. Reason: the dialogs replace cosmic-osd's own, and keeping passwords in one trusted program keeps the dialogs free of secrets.
