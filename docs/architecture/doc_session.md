# Athanor session lifecycle

Status: **revision 3 (2026-10-22) proposed, awaiting the maintainer's approval.** Revision 3 adds the optional session on Athanor's own compositor that `doc_own_stack.md` decision 10 and ADR-0122 decision 1 make conditional on an approved revision of this document, the greeter's session choice being its login path (AGENTS.md, "Stop and ask before editing"): the facts it rests on (section 1), SN14 to SN16, one exception of SN6, the tests of SN12, step 6 of SN13, questions 10 and 11 of section 4, acceptance items 9 to 16, and section 6, which records the maintainer's decisions of 2026-10-22 and the draft's recommendations still to approve. Until it is approved, revision 2 is in force and nothing of revision 3 is implemented (ADR-0074, rule 5). Revision 2 was approved by the maintainer on 2026-10-08. Revision 2 settles the six proposals of revision 1 (the single binding `PartOf=graphical-session.target`, which reverses LP2; the guards' back-off and its reset; no lock on a guard's failure; the notice and report figures; `athanor-autostart.service`; the variables never imported), and adds the case where `athanor-shelld` itself is the failing unit. It is the specification that maintainer decision A2-20 (#156) asks for: one document owns the chain that starts and stops the user's desktop session, its environment, the order of its units, and what happens when one of them crashes. Before it, eight specifications amended that chain piecemeal and gave it two different `PartOf=` rules and seven crash policies (audit 2 of 2026-10-05, desktop report, findings 2, 3, 7, 13 and 15). From this revision the other specifications cite it instead of editing the session files themselves. It also owns the crash notices of maintainer decision A2-24 (#156) and the format of the "Needs" lines from which `scripts/verify.py docs` builds the cross-specification dependency graph.

- **How a session starts today** (branch `shell-specs` at `da940ab8`, read 2026-10-06).
  1. greetd runs `athanor-session` as the user (`forge/specs/athanor-system-config/SOURCES/usr/bin/athanor-session:2-4`).
  2. It stops whatever a previous session left in the user manager: `systemctl --user stop athanor-session.target` and `reset-failed` (`athanor-session:11-14`).
  3. It exports `XDG_CURRENT_DESKTOP=Athanor:COSMIC`, `XDG_SESSION_TYPE=wayland` and `XDG_DATA_DIRS` with `/usr/share/athanor/cosmic-defaults` first (`athanor-session:18,19,25`), and runs `athanor-usbguard-hook unlock` (`:29`).
  4. It execs `cosmic-comp` with `/usr/bin/athanor-desktop` as the compositor's client, under `systemd-cat -t athanor-session` (`:33`).
  5. `athanor-desktop` reads the session class from logind (`forge/specs/athanor-system-config/SOURCES/usr/bin/athanor-desktop:29-33`) and imports `WAYLAND_DISPLAY DISPLAY XDG_CURRENT_DESKTOP XDG_SESSION_TYPE XDG_SESSION_CLASS` into the user manager (`:34`). It supervises `cosmic-greeter` (the locker) and `cosmic-idle` as its own children with a back-off that doubles up to 60 s and never gives up (`:36-102`), and execs `systemctl --user start --wait athanor-session.target` (`:104`).
  6. `athanor-session.target` has `BindsTo=graphical-session.target`, wants and follows `graphical-session-pre.target`, wants `xdg-desktop-autostart.target` and is ordered before it, requires and follows the readiness gate `athanor-desktop.service`, and wants `athanor-skel-sync`, `cosmic-bg`, `cosmic-settings-daemon` and `cosmic-osd` (`forge/specs/athanor-system-services/SOURCES/usr/lib/systemd/user/athanor-session.target:7-24`). Our shell is enabled under it by user presets (`WantedBy=athanor-session.target`, for example `forge/specs/athanor-bar/athanor-bar-1.0.0/data/athanor-bar.service:77`); `system/Containerfile:189` asserts the wants links of bar, dock and shelld.
  7. The readiness gate `athanor-desktop.service` is a oneshot with `RemainAfterExit=yes` that waits up to 10 s for a `wayland-info` round trip, with a 15 s start timeout (`athanor-desktop.service:17-23`). It carries no `PartOf=`.
- **How it stops today.** The logout is `systemctl --user stop athanor-session.target` (`athanor-desktop:14`; the bar's power menu calls `StopUnit` on it, `forge/specs/athanor-bar/athanor-bar-1.0.0/src/ui/logind.rs:17,86-92`). The wait returns, `athanor-desktop` exits, cosmic-comp exits with its client, greetd shows the greeter. `graphical-session.target` has `StopWhenUnneeded=yes` and the target binds to it, so it stops once `athanor-session.target` is gone (`/usr/lib/systemd/user/graphical-session.target` of systemd 258.11 on the image, read 2026-10-06), and every unit with `PartOf=graphical-session.target` stops with it.

## 2. Decisions

**SN1. What this document owns.** The environment of the session and its import list (SN2), the `PartOf=` and `WantedBy=` rule (SN3), the start order (SN4), the stop order (SN5), the crash classes (SN6), the guards' degraded modes and the idle daemon's persisted inhibitors (SN7), crash notices and local reports (SN8), the end state of `athanor-desktop` (SN9) and of `XDG_CURRENT_DESKTOP` (SN10), and the "Needs" graph (SN11); from revision 3, the sessions the greeter offers (SN14), the optional session on the own compositor (SN15) and the gaps it ships with (SN16). Another specification that adds a session unit states its class (SN6) and any `After=` edge it needs by citing this document; it does not edit `athanor-session`, `athanor-desktop`, `athanor-session.target` or `athanor-desktop.service` itself. A change to any of these is a revision of this document.

**SN2. The environment.**

- **Set for the user manager by environment.d,** before it starts any unit: `XDG_CURRENT_DESKTOP=Athanor:COSMIC` from `/usr/lib/environment.d/50-athanor-desktop.conf` (`forge/specs/athanor-system-config/SOURCES/usr/lib/environment.d/50-athanor-desktop.conf:7`), so that a portal activated before the import already finds the desktop name and chooses `athanor-portals.conf`; `XDG_DATA_DIRS` from `60-athanor-cosmic-defaults.conf` (below); and `GTK_IM_MODULE=simple` from `50-athanor-desktop.conf` (LN11). The drop-in and `athanor-session` carry the same value and change together (SN10).
- **Exported by `athanor-session`,** before it execs cosmic-comp, in this order:
  1. `XDG_CURRENT_DESKTOP` (SN10) and `XDG_SESSION_TYPE=wayland`, as today;
  2. `XDG_DATA_DIRS` with Athanor's defaults first, as today, because cosmic-comp is a child of this script and not of the user manager; the user manager gets the same value from `/usr/lib/environment.d/60-athanor-cosmic-defaults.conf` (`forge/specs/athanor-calmo/SOURCES/usr/lib/environment.d/60-athanor-cosmic-defaults.conf`);
  3. after `athanor-first-run apply-handoff` (LN7), `LANG` and `LANGUAGE` from AccountsService (LN6) and the `LC_*` variables of the region (LN7);
  4. `XCURSOR_SIZE` and `XCURSOR_THEME` from the GNOME keys (AX9);
  5. `GTK_IM_MODULE=simple` while no input-method engine is enabled (LN11).
- **The import list.** `athanor-desktop` imports into the user manager exactly: `WAYLAND_DISPLAY DISPLAY XDG_CURRENT_DESKTOP XDG_SESSION_TYPE XDG_SESSION_CLASS LANG LANGUAGE LC_NUMERIC LC_TIME LC_MONETARY LC_PAPER LC_ADDRESS LC_TELEPHONE LC_MEASUREMENT XCURSOR_SIZE XCURSOR_THEME` and, while the input method is enabled, `XMODIFIERS QT_IM_MODULES QT_IM_MODULE`, which `athanor-session` then exports (LN11). A variable the session did not set is not imported, so an unset `LANGUAGE` leaves the manager's value alone.
- **Never imported** (settled 2026-10-08) `XDG_DATA_DIRS` (environment.d covers the manager), `XDG_SESSION_ID` (units find their session through logind, LP2), `XDG_ACTIVATION_TOKEN` (one per launch, BR2), `DBUS_SESSION_BUS_ADDRESS` (the manager's own).
- **Removed at the end.** The readiness gate gains `ExecStop=` that runs `systemctl --user unset-environment` over the same list except `XDG_CURRENT_DESKTOP`, whose value is static and comes from environment.d as well (unsetting it would leave a portal activated between two sessions without a desktop name), so a unit started after the logout and before the next login never inherits a dead display. The list lives in one file read by both `athanor-desktop` and the gate, so the two never diverge.
  - Needs: LN6, LN7, LN11, AX9.

**SN3. One binding rule.**

- **`PartOf=graphical-session.target`** (settled by the maintainer on 2026-10-08, reversing LP2). A2-20 (#156) decides that there is one binding rule; the target it names is this document's choice, and it is the one every session unit shipped today already uses (section 1). It applies to every user unit Athanor ships for the session, the readiness gate included, and for every application unit the broker creates. This is the convention of systemd.special(7) ("Such services should have `PartOf=graphical-session.target`") and of the upstream units the session runs, such as `xdg-desktop-portal.service`, so one rule covers ours and theirs and one stop transaction stops them all. `athanor-session.target` keeps `BindsTo=graphical-session.target`, so the session stops when either target does.
- **`WantedBy=athanor-session.target`** in `[Install]`, enabled by a user preset, for every unit the session starts eagerly. A D-Bus-activated unit (`athanor-osd`, the portal backend) has no `[Install]` section.
- **Every unit that connects to the compositor** has `After=` and `Requisite=athanor-desktop.service`, as the bar, the dock and the launcher do today. A headless unit (shelld, sessiond, broker) has neither, so D-Bus activation works before the gate.
- **Rejected: `PartOf=athanor-session.target`.** The logout is a stop job on `athanor-session.target` (section 1), and a unit `PartOf=` that target stops in that job's transaction. `graphical-session.target` stops later, in a transaction of its own, once nothing needs it (`StopWhenUnneeded=yes`), and that transaction stops every unit `PartOf=graphical-session.target`, upstream ones such as `xdg-desktop-portal.service` included. `After=` orders two stops only when both units are shut down together (systemd.unit(5)), so a unit bound to `athanor-session.target`, shut down with the logout's own job while the units bound to `graphical-session.target` are still running, is not held back by its edges to them. Bound as LP2 writes it, the lock and the agent would stop before the applications and the portal frontend, the reverse of SN5. With one binding every unit stops in the second transaction and SN5's edges hold. This reverses LP2's text; the lock and the agent are not built yet, so no shipped unit changes.

**SN4. The start order.** Each edge is an `After=`; a `Wants=` is named where the edge must also pull the unit in.

- **The guards are ready when they serve.** `athanor-lock`, `athanor-polkit-agent`, `athanor-idle`, `athanor-broker` and `athanor-a11y-gate` are `Type=notify` or `Type=dbus` and report ready only once they lock, are registered with polkit, hold their timers, own their name or listen, so an `After=` edge to them means what it says.
- **The start threshold of ST5 holds** with these edges: the bar now waits for two more units. If it does not hold on the reference machine, the edge is kept and the guard's start is made faster; ST5 is not relaxed (ST2).
  - Needs: LP2, LP11, AX5, SD8, SD10.

**SN5. The stop order.** When two units with an ordering dependency are both shut down, systemd applies the inverse of the start order: a unit with `After=` on another is stopped first; units with no ordering between them stop simultaneously (systemd.unit(5), `After=`). Every session unit is shut down with `graphical-session.target` (SN3), so the edges of SN4 give a partial order, and only that order is guaranteed:

- an application unit stops before the broker, the portal frontend, the polkit agent and the lock, which are still there while it saves its work;
- a shell surface stops before the lock and the polkit agent, so a session that ends while locked never shows its content; the bar stops before `athanor-shelld`;
- every unit that connects to the compositor stops before the readiness gate, which unsets the imported environment (SN2);
- `athanor-autostart.service` stops before the broker; Orca stops before the a11y gate.

Units with no edge between them (an application and the bar, the wallpaper and sessiond, the guards among themselves) stop in parallel. An edge is added here only when a stop needs a unit that would otherwise be gone.

The stop of each unit is bounded by the manager's default stop timeout. An application that does not exit within it is killed; this is the declared cost of ending a session.

**SN6. Crash classes.** Every session unit Athanor builds belongs to one class, and its unit file says which in a comment that cites this rule. The exceptions are named after the table.

| Class | Members | Policy | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Guards** | `athanor-lock`, `athanor-idle`, `athanor-polkit-agent` (with the `SystemPrompter`), `athanor-broker` (A2-20, #156); `athanor-sessiond` and `athanor-a11y-gate` (maintainer decision 2026-10-06 (#156)) | `Restart=always`, `RestartSec=100ms`, `RestartSteps=5`, `RestartMaxDelaySec=30s`, `StartLimitIntervalSec=0`; after 60 s of healthy run the program sends `RESTART_RESET=1` (below); a degraded mode while down (SN7) | given up; a guard restarts for as long as the session lasts |
| **Surfaces and their services** | `athanor-bar`, `athanor-dock`, `athanor-shelld`, `athanor-launcher`, `athanor-osd`, Settings, the overview, the control center, `athanor-wallpaper`, `athanor-update-notify`, `athanor-a11y` (the watcher) | `Restart=on-failure`, `RestartSec=100ms`, `StartLimitBurst=10` over `StartLimitIntervalSec=600`; the program's own SH8 counter decides its degraded state after five failures in ten minutes on `CLOCK_BOOTTIME` | a second, different counter |
| **Activated helpers** | the portal backend `xdg-desktop-portal-athanor` | no `Restart=`: the next D-Bus call activates it again; systemd's default start limit cuts a tight loop and expires, so a later call succeeds | a give-up that lasts the session |
| **Session steps** | `athanor-desktop.service`, `athanor-skel-sync.service`, `athanor-appearance-reset.service` (`doc_session_daemons.md` SD16, amended 2026-10-15, #473) | oneshot, no restart; the gate's failure fails the target and so the login, which greetd reports | a restart |

- **Why sessiond and the gate are guards** (maintainer decision 2026-10-06 (#156)). sessiond reports every other crash (SN8) and holds the USBGuard notice; a reporter that gives up hides every later failure. The gate fails closed (AX5): while it is down no screen reader reaches the bus, which for a blind user is a session that cannot be used.
- **The back-off resets only on request.** In systemd 258 the restart counter that `RestartSteps=` reads is flushed only by a start that is not an automatic restart (`src/core/service.c:2988-2990`, tag `v258`), by `reset-failed` (`:5262`), or by the notification `RESTART_RESET=1`, new in 258 (`:4981-4988`; `man/sd_notify.xml:379-387`). It is not reset by a healthy run, as `athanor-bar.service:20-22` already notes for the surfaces. A guard that crash-looped once would otherwise wait 30 s at every later crash of the session, and ST5's 1 s recovery would fail. So every guard sends `RESTART_RESET=1` through `sd_notify` once it has run for 60 s since its last start, the healthy-run threshold of `athanor-desktop:41-45` (30 s ceiling and reset on request settled 2026-10-08). The manager accepts the message only from a unit whose `NotifyAccess=` allows it: `Type=notify` sets `main` when it is unset (systemd.service(5)), and with `Type=simple`, `exec` or `dbus` the default is `none`, so every guard unit is `Type=notify` or sets `NotifyAccess=main` explicitly. The lock's first restart stays at 100 ms (LP10).
- **Exceptions.** COSMIC's units are named below. `athanor-launcher.service` keeps `RestartSec=1s` with `RestartSteps=5` and `RestartMaxDelaySec=60s` (`athanor-launcher.service:23-25`) until SN13 step 1 puts it on its class's values. `athanor-ime.service` (LN11) runs `ibus-daemon`, which has no failure counter of its own: it takes the surfaces' unit values without the SH8 counter, and sessiond's notice comes at its start limit (SN8). `athanor-compositor.service` (SN15) has no `Restart=`: Wayland clients do not survive their compositor, so a restart would bring back a session with every window gone; its end ends the session instead, through the script that waits on it (SN15).
- **COSMIC's units and children** keep their policies until they leave (SD22, OD15): `RestartSec=2s` in the three units, and `athanor-desktop`'s supervisor, which already never gives up, for cosmic-greeter and cosmic-idle.

**SN7. Degraded modes of the guards, and the idle daemon's inhibitors.**

| Guard | While it is down |
|---|---|
| lock | cosmic-comp keeps the defunct lock: every output stays dark and locked, and the new instance takes the lock over (LP10) |
| idle | no automatic blank, lock or suspend; the lock before sleep is the lock's own and still works (SD18); the inhibitors come back with the restart (below) |
| polkit agent | polkit finds no agent for the session (fallback off, LP11); what the caller then receives is polkitd's (section 4, question 3); the session never falls back to another agent |
| broker | launches fail closed with the caller's toast (SD7); running applications are unaffected |
| sessiond | a blocked USB device stays blocked; inserted drives queue in udisks; no crash notice is shown until it is back, and it then reports what the manager recorded meanwhile (SN8) |
| a11y gate | readers cannot connect; Orca is restarted after the gate (SN4) |

- **No guard locks the session because it failed** (settled 2026-10-08: the compositor keeps the defective lock, LP10, so a screen already locked stays locked). A crash loop with a 30 s ceiling would lock the user out every 30 s. The notice of SN8 tells the user instead.
- **The idle daemon keeps its inhibitors across a crash** (A2-20, #156), as `athanor-shelld` keeps its unread notifications (ST5):
  - At every change it writes its table to `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/athanor-idle/inhibitors.json`, mode 0600, through a temporary file and a rename: for each inhibitor its cookie, the unique bus name of its owner, its application id, its kind (idle or suspend only) and its reason. The directory is the unit's `RuntimeDirectory=` with `RuntimeDirectoryPreserve=yes`, so it survives a restart and leaves with `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR` at the end of the login.
  - At start it restores each entry whose owner still has its unique name on the bus (`NameHasOwner`); unique names are never reused on a bus, so a present name is the same client. Other entries are dropped, each with one journal line at info. New cookies continue above the highest restored one, so an `UnInhibit` after the restart finds its inhibitor.
  - A file that does not parse is removed, the event logged at warning, and the daemon starts with no inhibitor.
  - Needs: SD4, SD5.

**SN8. Crash notices and local reports** (A2-24, #156). Core dumps stay off; `athanor-sessiond` tells the user and keeps a report the user can attach to "Report a problem" (A2-19, #161).

- **The source is the user manager,** not files the units write. sessiond subscribes to the manager and, for each unit of the table in SN6, takes `Result`, `NRestarts`, `ExecMainCode`, `ExecMainStatus` and `InvocationID` from the `PropertiesChanged` signal sent when the unit enters `SubState=auto-restart` or `failed`: the next run overwrites them within `RestartSec=`, so they are captured from the signal, never read afterwards. A failure is a run whose `Result` is not `success`. If a signal is lost or coalesced, the manager's own journal entries for the failure (`UNIT_RESULT`, `EXIT_CODE`, `EXIT_STATUS` and the invocation id) are the record sessiond reads instead (section 4, question 2). Rejected: reading the SH8 records of each unit's runtime directory, which only the programs with a counter write.
- **The notice,** through `org.freedesktop.Notifications`, at most one per unit per session: at the first failure of a guard; at the fifth failure in ten minutes of a surface or service (SH8's point); when an activated helper reaches its start limit. It names the component in the user's words ("The screen lock stopped unexpectedly and was restarted") and offers "Report a problem". When the failing unit is `athanor-shelld`, which serves `org.freedesktop.Notifications`, the notice cannot be shown at that moment: sessiond still writes the report, and raises the notice once the name is owned again (shelld is a surface-class unit and restarts within 100 ms). If `athanor-shelld` reaches its start limit and the name is not owned again in the session, sessiond keeps the undelivered notice (the report is already on disk) as a marker file in that same crash-reports directory, so that SD17's single write directory holds, and raises it at the next session start, once the name is owned.
- **The report.** For every failure sessiond writes `$XDG_STATE_HOME/athanor/crash-reports/<UTC time>-<unit>.txt`, mode 0600, through a temporary file and a rename: the unit, the image version from `/usr/lib/os-release`, the time, `Result`, the exit code or signal, `NRestarts`, and the last 50 journal lines of the failed invocation (`_SYSTEMD_INVOCATION_ID`). It keeps the 20 most recent reports and deletes older ones. Nothing is sent anywhere: the "Report a problem" flow shows the report to the user, who decides whether to attach it.
- **sessiond's confinement** (SD17) gains one write directory, `$XDG_STATE_HOME/athanor/crash-reports`, and read access to the user's journal.
  - Needs: SD1.

**SN11. The "Needs" lines and the dependency graph.**

- **The format.** A dependency of one requirement on another is a line of its own, inside the requirement, that begins with `Needs:` (after an optional list marker), followed by requirement identifiers separated by commas and a final full stop: `Needs: LP2, SD4.` An identifier is one to three capitals and a number, defined in exactly one specification of `docs/architecture/` as the bold head of a requirement (`**LP2.`). The line belongs to the nearest requirement head above it. It means: the requirement above cannot be built before the named ones are.
- **What `scripts/verify.py docs` checks:** that every `Needs:` line is inside a requirement and parses; that every identifier it names is defined exactly once; that no requirement needs itself; and that the graph has no cycle, naming one when it does. It reports the number of requirements and edges as a note.
- **Prose dependencies** written before this revision ("Needs the lock of ...", SD22) are not lines of this format and are not read. Each specification converts them at its next revision; until then the graph holds only what this document declares.

**SN15. The session on the own compositor** (revision 3; `doc_own_stack.md` decision 10).

- **The script,** `/usr/bin/athanor-compositor-session`, a POSIX shell script under `set -eu` as `athanor-session` is, shipped by the compositor's package so that the greeter offers the preview exactly where the compositor is installed. greetd runs it as the user; in order, it:
  1. stops what a previous session left, `systemctl --user stop athanor-session.target athanor-compositor.service`, and resets failed units, as `athanor-session` does (`athanor-session:13-14`);
  2. installs its exit handler: `trap` on `EXIT` runs it, and `trap` on `HUP` and `TERM` exits with status 129 and 143, which runs it in turn, so that every way out of the script ends the session: a start that fails, a failed readiness gate or target, the compositor's end, a logout, a signal from greetd. The handler runs `systemctl --user stop athanor-session.target athanor-compositor.service`, which returns only once both are inactive (a `stop` without `--no-block` waits for its jobs, systemctl(1)), so the greeter never starts on a GPU the compositor still holds; then `systemctl --user unset-environment` over SN2's import list except `XDG_CURRENT_DESKTOP`, the list the gate's `ExecStop=` removes (SN2), `WAYLAND_DISPLAY` among them, because the gate may never have started or stopped, and over `ATHANOR_PREVIEW_SESSION`, `XKB_DEFAULT_LAYOUT`, `XKB_DEFAULT_VARIANT` and `XKB_DEFAULT_OPTIONS`;
  3. exports what `athanor-session` exports (SN2), except `XDG_DATA_DIRS`, which only cosmic-comp, a child of `athanor-session`, takes from there; `XDG_CURRENT_DESKTOP` is SN10's value for every session; it reads `XDG_SESSION_CLASS` from logind as `athanor-desktop:29-33` does, without which file search stays off; it runs the USBGuard hook as an unlocked session does;
  4. sets in the user manager, through `systemctl --user set-environment`, `ATHANOR_PREVIEW_SESSION=1` and `XKB_DEFAULT_LAYOUT`, `XKB_DEFAULT_VARIANT` and `XKB_DEFAULT_OPTIONS` from the system's keyboard setting (decided by the maintainer on 2026-10-22, section 6, item 4): the `X11Layout`, `X11Variant` and `X11Options` properties of `org.freedesktop.locale1`, which `localectl` sets and shows, read with `busctl get-property`, not from the text of `localectl status`, which prints `n/a` for a field that is not set. A value that is empty, `n/a` or `(unset)` means unset, and its variable is not set. It then imports SN2's list less `WAYLAND_DISPLAY`, which the compositor sets, and `DISPLAY`, which nothing sets without X11 (SN16, gap 6);
  5. runs `systemctl --user start --wait athanor-compositor.service` as an asynchronous command and waits for it with the `wait` builtin. A shell runs the trap of a signal only after a foreground command has completed, but a trapped signal makes `wait` return at once, with a status above 128, and the trap runs next (bash(1), SIGNALS; POSIX.1-2024, Shell Command Language, 2.11), so greetd's `HUP` or `TERM` reaches the handler without waiting for the compositor. The command starts the compositor and, through its `ExecStartPost=`, the session, and returns once the compositor's unit is inactive again (systemctl(1), `--wait`), whatever the cause: a start that failed (no `READY=1` within `TimeoutStartSec=`, a readiness gate or target that failed), a crash or a kill, a stop at logout. This is the only start of the compositor, so a compositor that has died is never started again in the same session (SN6); the exit handler then ends the rest.
- **Why the script waits on the compositor.** The session must end when the compositor ends, and it can end on its own: a crash, a `SIGKILL`, a GPU removed. `PropagatesStopTo=` would not do it: systemd.unit(5) applies it to stop jobs, and a main process that exits by itself queues none. Waiting on the target would not either: the target stays active when the compositor dies. The unit whose end must end the session is the one waited on, which covers a crash and a logout alike. Rejected: a runtime drop-in `BindsTo=athanor-compositor.service` with `After=` on the target under `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/systemd/user/athanor-session.target.d/`. It works (systemd.unit(5), `BindsTo=`: a unit bound to another that "suddenly enters inactive state" is stopped too, and with `After=` the bound unit "strictly has to be in active state"), but a drop-in left by a preview killed before its handler would make the next default session's target require the compositor, and the runtime directory outlives a session while the user has another one open. Whether cosmic-comp's exit ends the default session is section 4, question 1, and revision 3 does not rely on it.
- **Why the session starts from the compositor's unit.** `ExecStartPost=/usr/bin/systemctl --user start athanor-session.target` runs once the compositor has sent `READY=1` (systemd.service(5), `ExecStartPost=`), so the gate's probe and every unit after it meet a compositor that serves. `systemctl start` waits for the target's job and exits non-zero when it fails, a gate that fails or times out included; then "the unit is considered failed" (systemd.service(5), `ExecStartPost=`), the compositor is stopped and the script's wait returns. A session that fails to start ends as a compositor that dies does. The gate has no `After=athanor-compositor.service`: "the execution of ExecStartPost= is taken into account for the purpose of Before=/After= ordering constraints" (systemd.service(5)), so the gate would wait for the compositor's start, which waits for the gate, until `TimeoutStartSec=`. Rejected: the script starts the compositor, then the target, then waits on the compositor with a second `start --wait`; a compositor that died between the two would be started again.
- **`athanor-compositor.service`,** a user unit of the compositor's package: `Type=notify`, `ExecStart=/usr/bin/athanor-compositor --drm`, and the `ExecStartPost=` above; the compositor sets `WAYLAND_DISPLAY` in the user manager once its socket listens, then sends `READY=1` (SN13 step 6). `TimeoutStartSec=20s`: the start timeout covers the whole start, `ExecStartPost=` included; the gate gives up within 15 s (`TimeoutStartSec=15`, `athanor-desktop.service`), so 20 s leaves 5 s for the compositor to send `READY=1` and lets a failing gate report its own failure; a compositor that hangs before `READY=1` is stopped at 20 s. `TimeoutStopSec=5s`: a compositor that has not ended 5 s after `SIGTERM` is hung and gets `SIGKILL` (systemd.service(5), `TimeoutStopFailureMode=`), so a failed start leaves at most about 25 s of black screen. `PartOf=graphical-session.target` (SN3): the bar's logout stops `athanor-session.target` (`logind.rs:17,86-92`), `graphical-session.target` stops once unneeded (section 1), the compositor with it, and the script's wait returns. `Slice=session.slice`; `NoNewPrivileges=yes` (`verify.py services`, TM8); no `[Install]`, so only the script starts it and the default session never does; no `Restart=` (SN6). It inherits the manager's environment when it starts, so it reads the `XKB_DEFAULT_*` values set in step 4: Smithay's `add_keyboard` builds its keymap from an empty `XkbConfig` in a context with no flags (Smithay `023b78b`, `src/input/mod.rs:603-608`, `src/input/keyboard/xkb_config.rs:5-7`), and libxkbcommon then takes each empty name from `XKB_DEFAULT_LAYOUT`, `XKB_DEFAULT_VARIANT` and `XKB_DEFAULT_OPTIONS` unless the context was created with `XKB_CONTEXT_NO_ENVIRONMENT_NAMES` (`xkbcommon.h` of libxkbcommon 1.13.2, lines 455-459, 784-786 and 807). It takes the seat through libseat's logind backend from the unit, outside the session's scope (section 4, question 10; section 6, item 8).
- **When the script itself is killed.** A `SIGKILL` of the script runs no handler. greetd ends the session when the script ends, and the greeter returns. Without lingering, the user manager stops once the user has no session left, and the compositor with it. With lingering (loginctl(1), `enable-linger`: the user manager is kept around after logouts), `athanor-compositor.service` stays active without its logind session, whose devices logind takes back when the session closes, so the greeter's compositor can take the GPU (acceptance item 15 checks it); the user's next sign-in stops it, in the preview script's step 1 or in `athanor-session`'s cleanup (below). What a restart of greetd does to a running session is the same for both sessions and outside this revision: if it ends the script with `HUP` or `TERM`, the handler runs; if it kills it, the case above applies.
- **One session target for both.** `athanor-session.target` keeps its edges, the presets that enable bar, dock and shelld under it, and autostart. Rejected: a second target, which the bar's logout, the presets and SN4's edges would each have to name.
- **Three units stay out** (recommended, section 6, item 5). `cosmic-osd`, `cosmic-settings-daemon` and `athanor-control-center-shortcut` gain `ConditionEnvironment=!ATHANOR_PREVIEW_SESSION`: systemd.unit(5) checks the condition against the manager's environment, where `athanor-desktop:37` already puts what localsearch's `ConditionEnvironment=` reads, and `!` negates it. `cosmic-osd` is the polkit agent until LP11's retirement, and under the own compositor its overlay surface is closed, since the allow-list has no line for it: an agent that registers and cannot draw leaves each request waiting on a prompt nobody sees. `cosmic-settings-daemon` serves COSMIC's components, which the preview does not run. `athanor-control-center-shortcut`, wanted by `athanor-session.target`, writes Super+C into cosmic-comp's shortcuts, which the own compositor does not read (SN16, gap 7). `cosmic-bg` draws on the background layer, which every client has, and starts in both sessions.
  - **The variable's life.** Only the preview's script sets it (step 4) and its exit handler removes it (step 2). A preview killed before its handler runs leaves it in the manager, so `athanor-session` gains, beside its own cleanup (`athanor-session:13-14`), the same `unset-environment` and the stop of `athanor-compositor.service`. The stop runs only when the unit's file, `/usr/lib/systemd/user/athanor-compositor.service`, is installed: `athanor-session` runs under `set -eu` (`athanor-session:9`), and `systemctl stop` of a unit that is not loaded exits non-zero, which on an image without the compositor's package would end the default session at its start. `unset-environment` of a variable that is not set succeeds and needs no guard. The variable does not depend on `XDG_CURRENT_DESKTOP`, so SN10's change at SD22 step 8 leaves it as it is; each condition leaves with its unit (`cosmic-settings-daemon` at SD22 step 8, `cosmic-osd` at OD17 step 5, the shortcut unit with cosmic-comp's shortcuts), and the variable with the last of them.
- **The allow-list it ships,** `/usr/share/athanor/compositor/privileged-clients`, has one line for each shipped shell client that draws on the top or overlay layer or binds a privileged interface (#683, widened): the bar's, as in the tree; the dock's, `/usr/bin/athanor-dock athanor-dock.service zwlr_layer_shell_v1 ext_foreign_toplevel_list_v1`; the control center's, `/usr/bin/athanor-control-center athanor-control-center.service`, and the launcher's, `/usr/bin/athanor-launcher athanor-launcher.service`, each with `zwlr_layer_shell_v1` and the other privileged interfaces the test of SN12 finds it binding. Nothing in the preview starts the launcher yet: no key reaches it (SN16, gap 7), and the bar's Launcher button opens COSMIC's launcher name (`launch.rs:474`); its line is there because the test of SN12 covers every shipped layer-shell client. No other client is granted anything, and none is granted `ext_session_lock_manager_v1` (SN16, gap 1).
- **What it starts:** the compositor, the readiness gate, `athanor-shelld`, `athanor-bar`, `athanor-dock`, `athanor-skel-sync`, `cosmic-bg`, `athanor-update-notify`, the autostart entries, and the control center when the bar's button calls it. **Not:** cosmic-greeter as locker and cosmic-idle, children of `athanor-desktop`, which is cosmic-comp's client and does not run here; `cosmic-osd`, `cosmic-settings-daemon` and `athanor-control-center-shortcut`; the launcher.
- **What it does not provide yet,** besides SN16: a keyboard layout of the user's own and its switch (the system's layout only, step 4); X11 applications; window actions and the workspace, input-source and tiling controls of the bar and the dock, which take COSMIC's protocols (OS5; section 4, question 11); the client's cursor (a square is drawn), deep colour, a second GPU (README, task 8).

## 6. The maintainer's decisions and the draft's recommendations (revision 3)

**Decided by the maintainer on 2026-10-22:**

1. **No fallback to cosmic-comp.** A preview that fails returns to the greeter (SN14).
2. **The choice is not remembered** (SN14).
3. **The preview is offered on every installation** (SN14).
4. **The keyboard layout comes from `localectl`**: the system's X11 layout, variant and options, the properties of `org.freedesktop.locale1` that `localectl` sets and shows, set in the user manager before the compositor starts (SN15); a user's own layout and its switch wait for OS5.

**The draft's recommendations, approved or changed with this revision.** The text above follows each; none is decided yet.

5. **A discriminator only the preview sets,** `ATHANOR_PREVIEW_SESSION`, keeps `cosmic-osd`, `cosmic-settings-daemon` and `athanor-control-center-shortcut` out of the preview, and `XDG_CURRENT_DESKTOP` stays SN10's in both sessions (SN15). The alternative, the three units in the preview, leaves an agent that cannot draw.
6. **The menu's form:** a menu button beside the password field, shown only when the preview is installed (SN14). The alternative: a gear button in a corner of the greeter, as GDM's.
7. **The preview's entry names its gaps** (lock, prompts, screen-reader commands, shortcuts) in one line, because nothing else in the session says it never locks (SN14).
8. **The compositor as a user unit** (SN15), as ADR-0122's "its unit passes `verify.py services`" reads, against the compositor as the script's child, as cosmic-comp is; subject to section 4, question 10.
9. **The entry is marked as a preview**: its name ends in "(preview)" and its line says "Preview" (SN14). The alternative: an entry that names only the compositor, as an equal choice.
