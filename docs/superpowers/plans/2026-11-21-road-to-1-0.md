# Road to 1.0: Implementation Plan

**Status:** agreed with the maintainer on 2026-11-21, together with the scope decisions in section 2. This plan orders work. It approves no specification text: code waits for its approved text, under ADR-0074. On 2026-11-27 a census of the approved specifications against the code and the open issues filed the work no issue tracked, and each step below now names its issue.

**Goal:** release 1.0 as fast as correctness allows. The priority order is Sulphur, then Sal, then Settings. The exit criterion is ADR-0141's "everything own": `forge/config/own-allow.txt` is empty (#1292), and the image ships no COSMIC code, no GTK and no Nautilus.

**Basis:** a weighted census of the 1.0 milestone and of the work no issue tracks, made at `origin/iso-v0` 29309029c. Weights are S=1, M=3, L=8, XL=20. Most sizes are estimates, not measurements.

Two facts were checked at the source, and both change the order the census suggested:

- **Settings does not wait for the file manager.** Settings' requirements on Sal are SL1 to SL9 (`doc_settings.md` section 7). The file manager's are SR1 to SR14 (`doc_own_stack.md` decision 39). They are separate sets.
- **Little of Sulphur's standard-protocol gap is left.** The gap list of 2026-11-11 named touch, drm-lease, alpha modifier, fifo, tearing control, background effect and the Xwayland keyboard grab, and each now appears in Sulphur's source. Colour management does not, and it leaves 1.0 (section 2).

## 2. Scope decisions of 2026-11-21

- **The keyring prompter (SystemPrompter) is in 1.0.** ADR-0141 decision 2 already says so. ADR-0138 and `release-1.0` section 3 place it in 1.1, and #1355 brings them in line. Its code waits for the LP12 cryptography plan to be approved and for spike L9.
- **Colour management and HDR come after 1.0.** Output stays at 8 bits, as decided on 2026-11-11, and colour gets a revision of its own after 1.0.
- **The milestone is tidied**, as the maintainer confirmed after reading the list:
  - #538 is closed as complete for 0.9, and #1402 tracks the OS19 acceptance of 1.0;
  - #1364 to #1369 stay open: they are the spike S1 items that never ran, to be run in later sittings (decision of 2026-11-20);
  - #446 moves to After 1.0, since its deadline is 2027-06-14;
  - #1122 is closed, done in #1232.

## 8. Throughput and estimate

**What sets the pace is capacity, not the critical path.** The limits are:
- one heavy build at a time on each host, the desktop and the laptop;
- ten open pull requests (ADR-0135);
- a merge card and an approval for each merge.

Lanes 1 to 3 are therefore kept busy at all times, and lane 6 fills the free slots.

**Measured pace:** 231 merges in the 12 days to 2026-11-21. Milestone issues closed at about 20 weight units a week. That count runs low, because the ports already done (the bar, the launcher, the control center and the OSD) had no issues.

**Estimate:** 9 to 12 weeks, so between late January and mid-February 2027. Approving the wave 0 texts quickly is what shortens it most.
