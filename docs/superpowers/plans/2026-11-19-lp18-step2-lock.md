# LP18 step 2, the lock the maintainer sees: Implementation Plan

**Status:** approved by the maintainer on 2026-11-19, with decisions M1 to M4 taken as recommended (maintainer decision, 2026-11-19). The lock carries the login password from Sal's field to `athanor-unlockd`, an authentication path (`AGENTS.md`, "Stop and ask before editing"), so each task's code is written against acceptance criteria agreed before it starts.

**Goal:** `athanor-lock`, written in Sal, locks the session on logind's `Lock` and before sleep, shows LP6's screen, unlocks through `athanor-unlockd` or on logind's `Unlock`, and comes back after a crash. It is installed but not enabled, beside the locker it later replaces, and the maintainer judges it on the reference laptop (LP18 step 2). Step 3, the switch, is a plan of its own.

**Architecture:** `forge/specs/athanor-lock` is a Sal client. A toolkit-free core holds the state machine of LP2 (unlocked, locking, locked, authenticating, unlocking), tested without a display (SH4). Around it are three thin edges:
- logind, over `zbus`, on a calloop source given to `sal::run_with` (`system/athanor-sal/src/driver.rs:359-361`);
- the `athanor-auth` client of `athanor-unlockd` (LP8), on its own socket source;
- the screen, Sal's session-lock role (`Shell::lock` and `unlock`, `driver.rs:926` and `:943`; the `locked` and `finished` callbacks, `:316` and `:322`; #1271).

The process has one thread when it confines itself (LP15); the logind connection's thread starts after (decision M3).

**Tech stack:** Rust; `athanor-sal` without its `gpu` feature (LP15); `athanor-auth` (`system/athanor-auth`, LP18 step 1, #1025); `zbus` from the workspace; calloop through Sal. No new crate in `Cargo.lock`.

**Spec:** `docs/architecture/doc_lock_and_prompts.md`, revision 4 (#1019), with the following:
- LP1 to LP6, LP8, LP10, LP14, LP15, LP17, LP18 steps 1 and 2;
- D24 to D38;
- section 4, spikes L4 and L5;
- section 5, the items that step 2 can run.

Also `docs/decisions/0141-everything-own-in-1-0.md`, decisions 3 and 6; `doc_own_stack.md` OS4 and OS5; `doc_accessibility.md` AX4 and AX14.

## Global constraints

- Sulphur is the only session (ADR-0141 decision 3). The plan drops every clause of `doc_lock_and_prompts.md` about cosmic-comp's session. The amendment that says so is listed under "Changes owed".
- The password leaves the field only as a borrow (`Ui::password_text`, `system/athanor-sal/src/widget.rs:1147`), is copied once into an `athanor-auth` zeroizing buffer, is written to the socket, and both buffers are cleared at once (LP1). Nothing logs it, and no `Debug` shows it.
- No new `unwrap`, `expect` or `panic!` outside tests (`verify.py panics`). The unit sets `NoNewPrivileges=yes` (TM8, `verify.py services`).
- Each prerequisite and each task below is a pull request of its own (LP18: "each step merges on its own"). The Sal ones carry no Athanor dependency (ADR-0126).

## What already holds (read on 2026-11-19 at 89753530b)

- **Step 1:** `athanor-unlockd` and `athanor-auth` merged with #1025 (D24 to D33).
- **The session-lock role:** Sal's driver has it (#1271).
- **The password field (AX4):**
  - The editor holds only masks. AT-SPI, the selection, Copy, Cut and the edit menu give out masks or nothing (`widget.rs:5351-5421`).
  - The text lives apart in `password::Secret`. Every change rebuilds it in a buffer of exactly its size, and the old buffer is zeroized (`system/athanor-sal/src/password.rs`). The spec asks for "a fixed zeroizing buffer handed out only as a borrow". This mechanism differs, but it reaches the same end: no freed buffer holds the text. The allocator probe of `system/athanor-sal/tests/password_memory.rs` already checks that end.
- **The keyboard-group protocol:** Sulphur serves `athanor_keyboard_group_manager_v1` (`forge/specs/athanor-sulphur/athanor-sulphur-1.0.0/src/keyboard_group.rs`), and the bar is its one client today.

## Prerequisites in Sal (D36, D38), before step 2

### Task 0a: a password field for a trusted surface

Two rules are not met yet:
- D38: the lock's field enables no text-input, so its keys come from `wl_keyboard` alone. Today a focused password field enables `zwp_text_input_v3` with `HiddenText | SensitiveData` (`ime_field`, `driver.rs:1970-1981`, enabled by `sync_text_input`, `:2381-2395`), and keeps what the input method commits (`widget.rs:5405-5412`).
- D36: the lock's field has no clipboard and no selection. Today a password field takes Paste (`widget.rs:5398-5403`), which the Wi-Fi dialog wants, and keeps a selection over its masks that Copy and Cut act on.

**Proposed:**
- A constructor `Ui::trusted_password(parent, name)` sets a flag on the field. Such a field:
  - is never `ime_field`, so the driver disables text-input while it has the focus;
  - refuses Paste, from the key and from the menu, where the item becomes insensitive;
  - refuses Copy and Cut the same way, and keeps no selection: Select All and a drag or Shift move only the cursor;
  - refuses an assistive technology's SetValue (Decision M2).
- `Ui::password` keeps today's behaviour for the Wi-Fi and VPN dialogs.
- Tests: driver tests that the text-input is disabled while such a field has the focus and enabled again on a plain field, and widget tests for Paste, Copy, Cut, the selection, the menu and SetValue.

### Task 0b: the active layout's name (LP14's chip)

Sal names no layout today. Sulphur's `athanor-sulphur-client` does (`system/athanor-sulphur-client/src/keyboard.rs:163-172`), but Sal may not depend on it (ADR-0126).

**Proposed:**
- Sal reads the layout names from the `wl_keyboard` keymap that sctk's xkbcommon state already holds, and the effective group from `modifiers`.
- It offers `Shell::keyboard_layout() -> Option<(u32, String)>` and an `Application::layout_changed` callback.
- The switch itself is not Sal's: the lock binds `athanor_keyboard_group_manager_v1` as a privileged client (task 6).
- Tests: a keymap fixture with two layouts, `us` and `it`, and a group change.

## Step 2

### Task 1: the crate, the unit and the package

- `forge/specs/athanor-lock/athanor-lock-1.0.0`, a member of the root workspace, packaged by `athanor-lock.spec`.
- `athanor-lock.service`, a user unit:
  - `PartOf=graphical-session.target`, in `session.slice` (LP2, SN3);
  - `Restart=always`, `RestartSec=100ms`, `StartLimitIntervalSec=0` (LP10, SN6);
  - `ProtectSystem=strict`, `ProtectHome=read-only`, `PrivateNetwork=yes`, `NoNewPrivileges=yes` (LP15).
- **Not enabled:** no preset line in this step, as the step's text says ("installed beside … but not enabled").
- `forge/config/packages.json` places the RPM in the image. `verify.py services`, `pam`, `toolkit-boundary` and `boundary` pass.

### Task 2: the state machine, toolkit-free

`src/state.rs` holds the states of LP2.

**Inputs:**
- logind `Lock` and `Unlock`, `PrepareForSleep(true/false)`;
- Sal's `locked` and `finished`, and every lock surface presented;
- the verdict of `unlockd`: `SUCCESS`, `FAILURE`, `UNAVAILABLE`, an end of stream;
- `LockedHint` read at start.

**Outputs:**
- lock, unlock, `SetLockedHint(true/false)`;
- take or release the sleep inhibitor;
- start a transaction, clear the field.

**Rules, one test each:**
- The session counts as locked only after `locked` (LP3).
- `finished` waits 1 s and asks again (LP3).
- `unlock_and_destroy` is sent on exactly three paths: the lock's `SUCCESS` and logind's `Unlock` (LP3), and the driver's own answer to the compositor's `finished` (`driver.rs:2974-2982`), which the protocol asks for and which the 1 s re-lock follows. A test for each, and one that nothing else reaches `Shell::unlock`.
- The sleep inhibitor is released only after `locked` and a presented frame on every output (LP4, spike L4).
- A start with `LockedHint` true locks at once (LP10).
- An end of stream is "Authentication failed, try again" (LP10).
- The field stays insensitive for PAM's delay (LP6).
- No password is held between attempts (LP8).

### Task 3: logind

`src/logind.rs` is the only module with D-Bus:
- It finds the session through `Display` of `/org/freedesktop/login1/user/self`, checks `Type=wayland` and `User`, and exits non-zero otherwise (LP2).
- It subscribes to the session's `Lock` and `Unlock` and to the Manager's `PrepareForSleep`, and calls `SetLockedHint`.
- It takes the `sleep` inhibitor in `delay` mode, as LP4 words it.
- The connection runs on a thread of its own, started after confinement (task 5), on a current-thread Tokio runtime as `athanor-idle` and `athanor-sessiond` run theirs. It hands logind's events to the main loop over a channel and wakes it through a `calloop::ping` source handed to `sal::run_with`; the main loop's calls go back over a channel. No password crosses either channel.

Decision M3: the connection's thread, after Landlock.

Tests: the subscriptions and the inhibitor against a private `dbus-daemon` that the test starts, with no display.

### Task 4: the unlockd client

`src/unlock.rs`:
- It connects to `/run/athanor/unlockd.socket` and writes `athanor-lock` as the first line (D28).
- It answers `PAM_PROMPT_ECHO_OFF` with the field's text: a borrow, copied into `athanor_auth::buffer`, written, then cleared.
- It shows `PAM_ERROR_MSG` and `PAM_TEXT_INFO`, mapping them as LP6 does: PAM's text, else "Wrong password".
- It parses through `athanor_auth::protocol`.
- One connection per attempt, no retry of its own. The fingerprint connection is LP9's, in LP18 step 7.

Tests: against a stub server on a socket pair, the prompt-answer exchange, `FAILURE`, an end of stream, and an over-long line, which the protocol refuses.

### Task 5: confinement and start

`main.rs`, in this order:
1. Read the locale.
2. Apply Landlock: writes only under `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR`.
3. Set `PR_SET_DUMPABLE` 0.
4. Start the logind thread, which opens the connection.
5. Call `sal::run_with`.

Built without `gpu`, so no GPU node is opened (LP15). A test asserts that the process has one thread at the moment Landlock applies.

### Task 6: the screen

LP6 on the session-lock role:
- **The primary output** is the output of the first lock surface, which Sulphur gives the keyboard. It shows the time and date in the user's locale, the avatar and full name from AccountsService, task 0a's field with a Caps Lock warning, task 0b's layout chip, the seal (the trust-state file, else "Not verified"), battery and network read-only through `athanor-services` (CC3), and suspend, restart and power off through logind, the last two with confirmation in place.
- **Other outputs** show the time and date. Typing on any output goes to the field.
- **The chip** switches the group through `athanor_keyboard_group_manager_v1`, and the lock restores the group active at lock time when it unlocks (LP14).
- **Appearance:** the Hearth background, no accent colour, the four variants (LP14). Accessible names on every control, the focus on the field at lock, and "Screen locked" announced (LP6).
- **Sulphur's allow-list** gains `/usr/bin/athanor-lock athanor-lock.service ext_session_lock_manager_v1 athanor_keyboard_group_manager_v1` (LP3), checked by `verify.py allow-list`. cosmic-greeter's line goes in step 3, not here.

Decision M4: the accessibility menu, Super+Alt+A, at the lock.

### Task 7: the VM test and the judgement

The VM test in `scripts/devvm/acceptance`, with the unit started by hand as `athanor-unlockd`'s socket was (D30):
- `loginctl lock-session` locks it, and `LockedHint` turns true;
- the right password unlocks it, and a wrong one fails with the delay;
- `loginctl unlock-session` unlocks it;
- a SIGKILL while locked is followed by a lock surface on every output within 1 s (LP10, ST5);
- a suspend while unlocked resumes locked (LP4).

Spike L5 measures memory and times on the reference laptop with this build, and the unit's `MemoryMax=` is set at the measurement plus 15% (LP15, ADR-0068). Spike L4 runs with it. The maintainer then judges the lock on the reference laptop before the step merges (LP18).

## Decisions

The maintainer took all four as recommended on 2026-11-19 (maintainer decision, 2026-11-19).

1. **M1. cosmic-comp's session.** Under ADR-0141 decision 3, the lock is built and tested on Sulphur only. Recommended: yes, with a `doc_lock_and_prompts.md` amendment that drops the cosmic-comp clauses of LP3, LP6, LP10, LP14, D35 and spike L2. The alternative keeps the clauses until cosmic-comp's session leaves the greeter.
2. **M2. SetValue on the lock's field.** Recommended: refuse it. Any process of the user can reach the field through AT-SPI and submit guesses that count towards the lockout. Braille keyboards reach the lock as keys, so they are not cut off. The alternative keeps SetValue, as the Wi-Fi field does.
3. **M3. zbus's thread.** LP15 asks for one thread when the process confines itself. The first recommendation, `internal_executor(false)`, rested on an option of zbus 5: the workspace's zbus 6.2.1 has none, and runs a connection on Tokio or on a `Runtime` given to `connection::Builder::runtime`. *Revised by the maintainer on 2026-11-19:* the process confines itself with one thread, then runs the connection on a second thread (task 3). The alternative, a `zbus::Runtime` implemented over calloop, keeps one thread throughout at the cost of an executor of the lock's own.
4. **M4. The accessibility menu at the lock (F-lock-20, AX14).** Recommended: in step 2, since the lock replaces a locker the maintainer uses daily, and a screen-reader user must not lose it at step 3. The alternative is a pull request of its own before step 3.

## Review focus

- That the password never reaches a `String` of the lock's own code, a log line or `Debug`, and that every buffer that held it is cleared after the write.
- That `unlock_and_destroy` has no path but the three of task 2.
- That the process has one thread when Landlock applies, and that no password reaches the logind thread.
- That a crash at any state brings the lock back within 1 s.
